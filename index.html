What does the $39.90 payment include?

A one-time MOVP purchase for 1 production website and 1 related staging/test site, with 365 days of updates and support. There is no subscription and no automatic renewal.

What happens after 365 days?

Your installed plugin continues to work. The included updates and support period ends; renewal for further updates and support is optional and manual. Expiry or a licensing API outage does not switch off the installed plugin's core functionality.

Can I use it on a staging site?

Yes. The license covers 1 production website plus 1 related staging/test site. A separate shop or unrelated production domain is not a staging site.

Which setups are supported in version 1.0?

MOVP supports WooCommerce manual orders with HPOS or legacy order storage. WordPress Multisite is not supported in 1.0. WooCommerce Subscriptions and bundles/composites are currently not supported or tested.
